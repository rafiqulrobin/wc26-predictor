Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

LiveWC26 ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit livewc26.com.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information only when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to match result notifications

We do not collect any payment information, passwords, or sensitive personal data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you match result email notifications (only if you subscribed)

Cookies and Analytics

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We may use basic analytics to understand how users interact with our site. This data is anonymized and does not identify individual users.

Our site contains affiliate links to VPN providers (including NordVPN and Surfshark). When you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are useful to our users.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytical purposes.

Email Communications

If you subscribe to our email notifications, you will receive match result emails sent from updates@livewc26.com. You can unsubscribe at any time by contacting us at hello@livewc26.com.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
